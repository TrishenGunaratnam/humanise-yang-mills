## Input contract and notation

The mathematical input `eq:trace-sensor-chart` is exactly the conclusion of `YM.Trace.Terrace.lensP`, not a newly assumed sensor estimate: its limiting section is the componentwise grade-five `ladder`, and its chosen maps have the component formula below, converge pointwise to the section, and are eventually right inverses simultaneously for every vector. The source supplies this chart under the parameters in `springs`: `hZ : steep 𝓛 Z`, `H : C.Makes`, `hb : (𝓛 : Filter (ℕ × ℕ)) ≤ F.fuse`, `hr : Scrim C W u ψ φ δ ζ r D`, `hq : Gondola F a X 𝓛 V Y`, `K : Sceptre C W`, `θ : PostT C.path.levels`, `hG : G.Simple`, and `hp : Spun Z X V a s`. These are structured assumptions, not asserted to follow just from the abstract identities in the manuscript. `Gondola.growth` is the selected-level divergence; `Gondola.terminal` supplies eventual grade-five lift convergence along the fixed ultrafilter and `X.U i 5 z = S.project 5 (Y 5 i z)` for every field `z`. `Spun.starts` identifies the sensor on a common tail, component by component, with the normalized Vault map. The source's finite-volume/scale chart production has not been independently audited in this unit; the above is the precise interface consumed.

The second input `eq:trace-sensor-marked` is **exactly** the three extra arguments of `springs`, for an arbitrary sequence `v`: convergence to `Stem5`, `hueK(A)(Stern V i) • s i (v i) → 0`, and `hueK(A)(Stern V i) * Lease (U#) hG (v i) → 0`. There is no requirement that `v` be the medium or that the resulting functionals converge in the statement of `Prosper`. The special medium is one later supplier, not a restriction on `springs`. Pointwise inequalities in `Prosper.vote` have one eventual index uniform over vectors; the weighted bound has a tail that may depend on the positive epsilon. Both are preserved.

## Literature and editorial decisions

Searched `blueprint/background/` and relevant `Bots/notes/` for trace, receiver, finite-dimensional perturbation and Ward results; no accepted matching external receiver theorem was located. Checked M. Winklmeier, *Functional Analysis*, Theorem 2.10, p. 29, https://math.uniandes.edu.co/~mwinklme/teaching/FuncAna/FA-lecture_notes.pdf : on a Banach space `||T||<1` makes `id−T` invertible by the Neumann series with inverse norm bounded by `(1−||T||)⁻¹`. Finite-dimensional `E` is Banach, and the manuscript applies it to `T=id−T_i` after norm convergence; the specific rank-one/weighted argument is the existing proved manuscript lemma, not a claim that this reference proves it. No extra bibliography dependency is needed for the elementary geometric-series step, whose application is explicit. This unit groups the source's finite-basis continuity, decomposition, triangular repair, and operator-norm extraction by mathematical purpose.

The three displayed curvature-jet tests give evaluation rows `(0,2,0)`, `(1,2,0)`, `(1,2,1)` on the raw vectors `momentum1`, `momentum2`, `eom`. Their derivative jets obey the cyclic Bianchi identity, and the displayed polynomial potential realizes them. Together with the parent free-rank upper bound three, they prove the raw basis used in `bareCoordinates`; these tests are a reorganization, not a verbatim transcription of the source's `test_m` and `test_n`.

The [notation protocol](../../../docs/notation.md) and [shared glossary](../../glossary.md) govern integration. Here `a_i` is the `1/5` age at the postlevel; it must not be confused with the parent chapter's local uses of `a_i` as another scale. The chart normalization `c_i` is distinct from `a_i` and `b_n`. No shared glossary edit is made. The parent manuscript already owns the receiver proof, the free rank, and the scalar medium argument; they are cited rather than duplicated.
